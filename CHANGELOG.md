# Changes since the September cut

The [README](README.md#status) describes the system as it stood on 21 September 2026, and the
kit in `AGENT.md` and `skills/` is a distillation of that date. The system it came from kept
changing. This file lists what changed in how it runs, by the date it happened, and where each
change is written up. The kit stays as it was unless an entry says otherwise.

## 11 November 2026

**The center was rewritten around five families of repeated correction, and twelve days later
the correction rate had not visibly moved.** A count of one month of my corrections showed
that every family already had written rules, 65 places in all, and that 8 of them sat where
the assistant reads in every conversation. On 29 October the top of the center became five
directions, one per family. Counted again on 11 November with the same judge, the rate was
7.45 corrections per 100 messages against 5.97 before, a difference that cannot be told from
chance. The first judgment, made by a different judge, had shown a drop of 29 percent.

Written up in
[`METHOD.md`, section 11](METHOD.md#a-rewrite-of-the-center-measured-before-and-after).

For the kit: `AGENT.md` stays as it is. The five directions answer my own corrections, and
they have yet to show an effect.
